# Project 3

## Dataset
The final project dataset is sourced from IMDB. For the purpose of this project, we trimmed down the dataset to include only movies, actors, crew, directors, etc. from the US since 1960.

The original dataset and specification can be found [here](https://developer.imdb.com/non-commercial-datasets).

For the smaller version of the dataset, click [here](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1cqqr7wb__IHZqsMDLXKy8oNIDfSPAf00?usp=sharing).

## Favorite Movie

Your code (and comments) should be able to answer the following questions about your favorite movie in the dataset:
1. What is the title, identifier, and release date of your movie?
2. What is the IMDB rating?
3. Who are the writers? (and identifiers)
4. Who are the actors? (and identifiers)
5. Who is the director? (and identifier)
6. What other movies did the director also direct?
7. What other genres does this director work on?
8. What is the average rating of all the movies by this director?
9. Is your favorite movie by this director higher or lower rated than the average? Do you agree? Why or why not?
10. **Extra Credit:**
Make a short slideshow presentation of your favorite facts and work from any part of this project. We'll have an informal presentation day for everyone on the last day of class.
